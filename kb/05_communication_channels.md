# Customer Communication Channel Policy

Customers can reach support through the in-app chat, the in-app call button, and email. In-app chat is the default channel and is used for all P3 and P4 tickets because it keeps a written record. Phone calls are reserved for P1 and P2 tickets where real-time back-and-forth is faster, and any phone resolution must be summarised back into the ticket in writing. Agents must never move a conversation to a personal number or an outside messaging app, since that breaks the audit trail and the customer's privacy protections.
