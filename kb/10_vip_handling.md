# VIP Customer Handling Policy

VIP status applies to a small set of long-tenure, high-frequency customers flagged in the account system. VIP tickets are automatically raised one priority level above their normal classification, so a VIP P3 issue is handled as a P2. These tickets are routed to experienced agents and, where compensation applies, the front-line approval cap is doubled. VIP status affects speed and handling only; it never changes the factual answer to a policy question, and non-VIP customers receive the same accurate information.
