# Feedback Collection Process

After a ticket is marked Resolved or Closed, the customer is sent a short satisfaction survey through the same channel the ticket used. The survey asks for a 1-to-5 rating and an optional comment, and it stays open for 7 days before it expires. Ratings of 1 or 2 automatically create a follow-up review so a supervisor can check whether the ticket was really resolved. Feedback is aggregated weekly to spot recurring pain points, and no customer is contacted again about a survey they chose not to answer.
