# SLA by Severity Policy

Each priority level carries a response-time and a resolution-time target. P1 tickets get a first response within 15 minutes and a resolution target of 4 hours. P2 tickets get a 1-hour first response and a 24-hour resolution target, P3 tickets get a 4-hour response and a 3-day resolution target, and P4 tickets get a next-business-day response with no fixed resolution deadline. If a resolution target is about to be missed, the ticket is automatically escalated one level so a senior agent can step in before the SLA breaches.
