# Escalation Matrix

Tickets escalate along a fixed three-tier path: Tier 1 front-line agents, Tier 2 specialist agents, and Tier 3 the operations lead. A ticket moves from Tier 1 to Tier 2 when it needs domain knowledge the front-line agent does not have, or when its SLA resolution target is at risk. It moves from Tier 2 to Tier 3 only for safety incidents, suspected fraud, or repeated failures of the same fix. Each escalation records who escalated it and why, so the history is auditable and nothing is escalated silently.
