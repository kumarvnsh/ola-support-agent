# Refund and Compensation Policy

Refunds are issued when a customer is charged for a service they did not receive, such as a cancelled ride that was still billed or a duplicate charge. Approved refunds return to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days, and to the Ola Money wallet within 24 hours. Goodwill compensation, such as ride credits, may be offered for service failures that did not involve an incorrect charge, capped at a per-incident limit that front-line agents can approve without a manager. Anything above that cap needs Tier 2 approval and a documented reason.
