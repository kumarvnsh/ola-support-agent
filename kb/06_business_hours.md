# Business Hours and Holiday Support Policy

Standard support hours for P3 and P4 tickets are 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM IST, seven days a week. Safety-critical and P1 tickets are handled 24 hours a day, every day of the year, including public holidays, because a stranded rider cannot wait for the next business day. On national holidays the non-critical queue runs with a reduced team, so P3 and P4 response targets are extended by one business day. The support calendar lists the specific holidays each year and is the single source of truth for these exceptions.
