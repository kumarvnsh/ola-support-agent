# Repeat Complaint Handling Policy

A complaint is treated as a repeat when the same customer reports the same underlying issue within 30 days of a previous ticket being closed. Repeat complaints skip the normal first tier and go straight to a Tier 2 specialist, because the first fix clearly did not hold. The specialist must look at the earlier ticket's history before responding, so the customer is not asked to explain everything again. Three or more repeats of the same issue trigger a root-cause review by the operations lead rather than another one-off fix.
