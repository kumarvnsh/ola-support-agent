# Outage Communication Protocol

When a service outage is confirmed, support switches to a single shared holding message so every affected customer gets the same accurate status. Agents must not speculate about the cause or promise a fix time that engineering has not confirmed. Tickets opened during a known outage are tagged to that incident so they can be resolved together once service is restored, rather than handled one by one. A final update is sent to all tagged tickets when the outage is cleared, along with any compensation that applies under the refund and service-credit policies.
