# Service Credit Policy

Service credits are small ride-fare discounts applied to a customer's account as an apology for a confirmed service failure. A credit is granted only when the failure is verified in the ticket record, never simply because a customer requests one. Each credit has an expiry of 30 days from issue and can be used on any future ride, but only one service credit may be active on an account at a time. Credits are compensation, not refunds, so they are tracked separately and never returned as cash to a payment method.
